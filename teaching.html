Teaching

Allison has taught corporate financial reporting to more than 3,700 graduate and 120 undergraduate students at Georgetown’s McDonough School of Business. She is also the creator of the Corporate Financial Reporting course offered in MSB's full-time, part-time, and online MBA Programs.
