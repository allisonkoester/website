Allison Koester

Associate Professor of Accounting
Dean’s Research Fellow
Georgetown University · McDonough School of Business · Washington, DC (USA)

Allison Koester is an Associate Professor of Accounting and a Dean’s Research Fellow at Georgetown University’s McDonough School of Business.

Her research considers how taxation, government financial assistance, legislation, and regulation shape corporate economic activity and outcomes. Her work has appeared in The Accounting Review, the Journal of Accounting and Economics, The Journal of Law and Economics, Management Science, and the Review of Accounting Studies. Her research has also been cited in Securities and Exchange Commission releases and a Government Accountability Office report, and has covered by news outlets including the Wall Street Journal. She has presenter her research at invited presentations at more than 100 university seminars and academic conferences.

She has taught corporate financial reporting to more than 3,700 students in Georgetown’s MBA and Executive MBA programs. She earned her Ph.D. at the University of Washington, and prior to entering academis she worked as a tax consultant at KPMG (Washington DC & Northern Virginia).
