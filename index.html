Allison Koester

Saleh Romeih Associate Professor of Accounting
Dean’s Research Fellow
Georgetown University · McDonough School of Business

Allison Koester is the Saleh Romeih Associate Professor of Accounting and a Dean’s Research Fellow at Georgetown University’s McDonough School of Business.

Her research considers how taxation, economic incentives, legislation, and regulation shape corporate economic activity and outcomes. Her work has appeared in The Accounting Review, the Journal of Accounting and Economics, The Journal of Law and Economics, Management Science, and the Review of Accounting Studies. It has been cited in Securities and Exchange Commission releases and a Government Accountability Office report, and covered by outlets including the Wall Street Journal. She has presented her research at more than 90 academic institutions and conferences.

She has taught corporate financial reporting to more than 3,700 students in Georgetown’s MBA and Executive MBA programs. She earned her Ph.D. at the University of Washington and previously worked as a tax consultant at KPMG.
